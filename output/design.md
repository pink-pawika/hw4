# Design

The site is styled as the Campus Customs shop on Broadway, not as a plain catalogue.

- **Sign.** The header leads with “Campus Customs” in Fraunces, with “57 Broadway, New Haven” under it. The old stack put “Yale” in the biggest type. Shoppers should see whose door they are at.
- **Awning.** The top bar is navy with a thin stripe, the way a store awning reads from the sidewalk. It still says Yale hoodies, crewnecks, and college shirts.
- **Window.** The home headline sits in a navy panel with a brass strip under it. Fraunces is only for the sign, headlines, and prices. Source Sans 3 carries the rest, so the page is easy to scan.
- **Price tags.** Each price is a small navy ticket. On a card, the name is first and the ticket is second, so the number to pay is obvious before the description.
- **Walls.** The page is warm paper, and product photos sit on a lighter wash so navy garments do not disappear. Filters, the chat button, and the footer keep the same navy and brass.

People stay when the page feels like a place they could walk into. They are more likely to buy when the price is a tag, not a line of text they have to hunt for.
